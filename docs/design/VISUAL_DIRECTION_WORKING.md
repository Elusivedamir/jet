# Visual and UX direction — working exploration

**Status: provisional, non-binding.**

The assistant leads initial visual exploration and proposes screens, interaction flows, visual language and component concepts. The owner has explicitly said not to bind the project to the first design.

- The design may change after usability review, product research and implementation evidence.
- No visual concept, palette, typography, component library or layout is a permanent constraint yet.
- Do not let a mockup dictate backend architecture or data contracts.
- Preserve the approved product actions and business plan.
- Explore alternatives where they materially improve clarity, accessibility or usability.
- A mockup is not a working feature and must never be reported as one.

The assistant should make sensible initial design decisions independently, show concrete drafts when useful, and keep them easy to revise.
